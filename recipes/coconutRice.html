coconutRice

Description

This coconut rice is best served with chicken curry, ngapi kyaw, and roselle leaves. Serve and enjoy!

  • 3 cups uncooked jasmine rice, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • 1 tablespoon grated onion, or to taste
  • 1 pinch white sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
